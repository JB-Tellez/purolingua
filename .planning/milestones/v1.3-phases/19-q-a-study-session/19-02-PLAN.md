    - `normalize(s)`: `s.toLowerCase().trim().replace(/[.,!?;:'"¿¡]+$/g, '').trim()` — same as Rephrase

    - allScenariosDone screen: heading `t('allScenariosDone')`, same two buttons
    - `resetSession()`: resets index=0, done=false, allDone=false, selectedChoice=null, micState='idle', feedbackState=null. Does NOT call `useState(() => ...)` again — the snapshot is taken at mount only; Study again re-uses the same snapshot (same behavior as Rephrase)
